# Evaluating the Effectiveness of a Digital Workload Management Information System (DWMS)

This repository contains the published research paper and supplementary materials for the study:

**“Evaluating the Effectiveness of a Digital Workload Management Information System in Enhancing Organizational Management Efficiency: A Case Study of the Faculty of Engineering, Mahasarakham University”**

## Abstract
This study evaluates the effectiveness of the Digital Workload Management Information System (DWMS) in improving organizational management efficiency through proactive maintenance, digital workflow integration, and organizational data management.
